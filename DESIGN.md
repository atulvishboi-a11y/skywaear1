# DESIGN.md — ZENIN Obsidian Streetwear Design System

## Core Aesthetic & Philosophy
- **Identity**: Obsidian Brutalism. Tactical, industrial, high-precision technical streetwear.
- **Strict User Mandate**: 
  - **NO sloped or blurry AI gradients**: Solid, pure, high-contrast colors only.
  - **NO rounded corners or curves**: Zero border-radius (`rounded-none`, `0px`) on all cards, buttons, badges, inputs, and modals. Plain, solid, perfect edges.
  - **Industrial Streetwear Grid**: Crisp 1px `#282C34` borders, monospace metadata stamps, sharp rectangular layout containers.

## Color Palette (Solid, High-Contrast)
- **Background Root**: `#0B0C10` (Obsidian Black)
- **Surface Card**: `#16181D` (Solid Dark Slate)
- **Sub-surface / Alt Card**: `#1F2128` (Solid Charcoal)
- **Border Primary**: `#282C34` (Industrial Grey)
- **Border Active / Highlight**: `#3E4451` / `#F5F5F7`
- **Accent Primary (Lime)**: `#00FF66` (Solid Cyber Lime — for badges, active states, key CTAs)
- **Accent Secondary (Cyan)**: `#00F0FF` (Solid Cyan — for technical specifications like `[240 GSM]`)
- **Text Primary**: `#F5F5F7` (Pure Off-white)
- **Text Muted**: `#8E929B` (Cool Concrete Grey)

## Typography
- **Headings & Badges**: Space Grotesk (Bold, uppercase, crisp tracking)
- **Technical Specs & Meta**: Space Mono / Monospace (`[240 GSM]`, `[SYSTEM ACTIVE]`)
- **Body & Editorial**: Work Sans (Crisp readability, no serif distractions)

## Structural Rules
- **Corner Radius**: 0px everywhere (`rounded-none` / `border-radius: 0px`).
- **Divider Lines**: Solid 1px `#282C34`, no blurred drop shadows or soft ambient glows.
- **Buttons**: Sharp rectangular blocks with high-contrast text and instant hover states.
- **Imagery**: High-contrast, clean bounding boxes with technical tag overlays.
