# PRODUCT.md — ZENIN Heavyweight Anime Streetwear

## Platform
- **Type**: Web (Next.js App Router, React, Tailwind CSS)
- **Deployment**: Local dev / Node.js production server
- **Primary Audience**: Gen-Z and millennial streetwear & anime enthusiasts in India seeking premium heavyweight apparel with tangible rewards.

## Purpose & Positioning
ZENIN is an Indian direct-to-consumer (D2C) anime streetwear brand offering custom 240+ GSM heavyweight drop-shoulder oversized t-shirts. 

### Meaningfully Different Mechanism
- **10% Cashback Wallet with Vesting**: Every purchase earns 10% cashback in ZENIN Coins directly into the user's Live Wallet, vesting weekly to encourage long-term customer loyalty.
- **Instant UPI Cash Withdrawal**: Coins can either be redeemed against future drops or withdrawn directly to UPI once vested.
- **Heavyweight 240 GSM French Terry / Super-Combed Cotton**: Premium handfeel, durable neck ribs, pre-shrunk, engineered drop-shoulder boxy silhouette.
- **Merchandised Anime Archives**: Organized by thematic anime sagas and limited drops.

## Target Audience & Situations
- Streetwear collectors looking for authentic oversized boxy fits rather than cheap fast-fashion merchandise.
- College students, anime fans, and creators wanting durable, wash-resistant puff & high-density screenprints.

## Core Capabilities
- Storefront with instant filtering by theme, size, and merchandising tags (Premium, New Drops, Bestsellers).
- Dynamic cart quoting with tiered coupon codes and reward coin redemption.
- Seamless customer dashboard with live order tracking, wallet balance, vesting breakdown, and UPI payout requests.
- Integrated WhatsApp concierge and order updates.

## Technical Constraints & Durables
- Solid, crisp brutalist aesthetics (zero border-radius, strict rectangular borders, no AI slope gradients).
- Preserves existing cart, auth, checkout, and wallet domain contracts.
